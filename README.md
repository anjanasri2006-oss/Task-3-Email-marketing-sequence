# 📧 Task 03 — Email Marketing Sequence

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of **SkillCraft Technology Task 03** and focuses on creating a professional **three-part email marketing sequence** for a fictional productivity brand.

The campaign is designed to guide potential customers through three stages of the marketing funnel:

**Awareness → Consideration → Conversion**

---

## 🏷️ Brand & Product

**Brand:** NexaNote  
**Product:** NexaNote Focus Planner  
**Campaign:** 3-Part Product Launch

NexaNote is a fictional productivity brand created for this project.

The **NexaNote Focus Planner** is a fictional digital productivity planner designed to help users organize priorities, break tasks into actionable steps, create focused work sessions, review progress, and maintain notes.

---

## 🎯 Target Audience

The campaign is designed for:

- 🎓 College students managing assignments, exams, projects, and deadlines
- 💼 Young professionals handling multiple work priorities
- 💡 Freelancers and creators organizing independent projects
- 📝 Individuals looking for a simple daily planning routine

---

## 📧 Email Marketing Sequence

### 1️⃣ Awareness — Email 01

**Objective:** Create awareness and introduce the problem and product.

The first email focuses on task overload and unclear priorities. It introduces the NexaNote Focus Planner as a simple way to organize priorities and identify the next practical action.

**Key Focus:**
- Identify a relatable productivity problem
- Introduce the product
- Communicate the product's value
- Create interest for the upcoming launch

---

### 2️⃣ Consideration — Email 02

**Objective:** Build trust and help the audience evaluate the product.

The second email explains how the Focus Planner works and highlights its key benefits. It also uses clearly identified fictional early-user feedback as social proof.

**Key Benefits:**
- ✅ Clearer priorities
- ✅ Simpler planning
- ✅ Visible progress
- ✅ Flexible use

---

### 3️⃣ Conversion — Email 03

**Objective:** Announce the product launch and encourage a specific action.

The final email announces that the NexaNote Focus Planner is officially available and provides one clear call-to-action.

**CTA:** `GET THE NEXANOTE FOCUS PLANNER`

---

## 📊 Marketing Funnel

| Stage | Goal | Email |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Capture attention and identify the problem | Email 01 |
| Consideration | Explain benefits and build confidence | Email 02 |
| Conversion | Encourage a specific action | Email 03 |

---

## 💡 Marketing Strategy

The campaign follows a progressive customer journey.

### Reader-First Messaging
The emails begin with the audience's productivity challenges before introducing the product.

### Clear Value
Each email communicates how the Focus Planner can help the audience organize priorities and take practical actions.

### Progressive Persuasion
Each email adds another reason for the audience to move toward the next stage.

### Single Primary CTA
The final email focuses on one clear call-to-action to avoid confusing the reader.

### Transparent Social Proof
Because NexaNote is a fictional brand, the early-user feedback is clearly presented as fictional.
